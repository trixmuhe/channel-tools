Channel Uploader

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Channel Uploader is a private tool. Its owner uses it only to upload, schedule and review videos on her own YouTube channels, and to read the view and subscriber counts of those channels.

Privacy policy

Data we collect: none. The tool is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it does not collect, store, share or sell personal data of any person.

Google user data: the tool uses YouTube access tokens for the channels of its owner. They are stored only on the private computer of the owner and are never shared with anyone. Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion: since no data of other people is stored, there is nothing to delete. For any question, write to the address below.
